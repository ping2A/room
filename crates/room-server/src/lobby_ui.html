How a netgame starts

  1. Host a gamePick Doom or Duke Nukem 3D, the map, skill and mode: co-op, deathmatch or DukeMatch. Your browser loads the game files locally.
  2. Share the linkFriends join from any browser, phones included, with the game link or its ID.
  3. FragLockstep netplay, voice chat and live spectating. Bots fill empty Battle Royale slots.
